Charts & activity

The categorical palette is not a rainbow. The first three series are the brand's own family — cyan, navy, amber — because a one- or two-series chart is the common case and it should look like this product. The ramp is tiered: slots 1–6 stay apart for a dichromat as well as in greyscale, and 7–12 are label-assisted — twelve saturated hues cannot all separate under red-green dichromacy, so a twelve-series chart needs its labels. On the navy paper every series lifts a tier, the same reason the focus ring switches to brand-400 on dark.

Every chart ships the numbers behind it as a visually hidden table. A picture of data is not readable by a screen reader, and a one-line summary is not the data. Unread is a dot plus weight, never a tint alone — a row slightly bluer than its neighbour is not a state anyone reads.
