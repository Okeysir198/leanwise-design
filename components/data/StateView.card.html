The five states

Empty, loading, error, offline and no-permission are one set. Shipping only the empty one is how the other four get invented per product — each with its own tone, its own icon, and its own idea of whether there is an action. These are the house answers.

Exactly one action, like EmptyState: a dead end with three buttons is a dead end with three ways to guess. The announcement differs by variant — error and offline use role="alert" because they interrupt; loading uses role="status" with aria-busy, because a progress report is not an interruption.
