Page grounds

Three grounds, one geometry. Honeycomb on the left, the mark on the right, a mirrored glow along the top. They differ only in what each commits to: .lw-page-light commits to the light ARTWORK (the ink lattice, the ink mark) over the page tokens and is deliberately not a band; .lw-page-dark is always navy and carries the dark band scope; .lw-page-ground swaps its artwork with [data-theme] on <html>, so a cached page is correct for every visitor.

Every value that differs is a custom property (glow stops, lattice alpha, comb pulse, mark alpha, sheen colour); the artwork URLs stay in explicit rules because Firefox resolves url() inside a custom property against the document, not the stylesheet.
