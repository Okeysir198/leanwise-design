Dialog, open

The overlays card can only show a closed dialog — the gates shoot what is on the page, and a panel that needs a click is never on it. This card mounts one Dialog already open, inside an OverlayProvider whose root is the pane, so what the a11y and visual gates measure IS the open state: centred in the fixed layer, over the .lw-backdrop scrim, with focus inside.

One root, and nothing focusable outside the dialog, on purpose. A modal sets aria-hidden on every sibling of its portal; a second pane with its own buttons — or a second open modal — would fail aria-hidden-focus here, and be right to.
