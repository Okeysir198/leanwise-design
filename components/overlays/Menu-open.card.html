Menu, open

Both panels are open on load (defaultOpen), which is what lets the browser gates measure them at all — a closed overlay is invisible to axe and to a screenshot. They coexist because both are non-modal: a modal menu would make the rest of the page inert and swallow the other trigger's click.

The dark pane is the proof. Its trigger sits in .dark.lw-band-dark, the panel portals to document.body (there is no OverlayProvider on this card), and it still paints on the dark surface — the .lw-layer it mounts in mirrors the trigger's scope. Drop that mirror and this panel renders light on navy.
