Popover & Menu

One floating surface. Menu, Combobox, DatePicker and every filter panel are Popover plus contents — so there is one shadow, one radius and one dismissal behaviour in the system rather than five that drift apart. Since v2.0.0 the surface is Radix Popover / DropdownMenu (non-modal): positioning, collision flipping, outside-press, Escape and focus return are Radix's, not ours.

What stays ours is the scope. The panel portals into the nearest OverlayProvider layer and mounts a .lw-layer that mirrors the trigger's theme and band class — open the dark pane's menu and it paints dark, on a light page, with no class on <html>. Focus moves with the highlight: arrows, Home/End and typeahead move real DOM focus, because a highlight without focus tells a screen reader nothing changed.
