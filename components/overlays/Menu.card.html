Popover & Menu

One floating surface. Menu, Combobox, DatePicker and every filter panel are Popover plus contents — so there is one shadow, one radius and one dismissal behaviour in the system rather than five that drift apart. It is promoted to the top layer, which is the only way a panel escapes an ancestor's overflow: hidden without a portal.

Dismissal is ours, not popover="auto"'s. Auto light-dismisses on pointerdown anywhere outside — including the trigger, so a click on an open trigger closes the panel and the trigger's own handler immediately reopens it. Ours knows about the trigger. Focus moves with the highlight: arrows, Home/End and typeahead move real DOM focus, because a highlight without focus tells a screen reader nothing changed.
