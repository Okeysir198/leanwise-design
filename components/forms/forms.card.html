Form controls

Field is the unit. Label, control, and ONE slot that holds either help text or an error — never both, because a field showing both is a field whose error is easy to miss. Field wires htmlFor, aria-describedby and aria-invalid so the control never has to.

Invalid is driven by the ARIA attribute, not a class. The attribute is what a screen reader reads, so binding the colour to it makes the two impossible to desync. The focus ring turns red too — a red border with a cyan ring reads as "already fixed" the moment it is focused.
