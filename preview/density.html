Density

Density is a second, semantic layer — not a remap of the space scale. The rule that makes the scale good (--lw-space-24 is 24px, always) is exactly what stops density living there: re-pointing it to 16px would break the one promise the token's name makes. So the px scale stays literal and is what you compose from; --lw-control-h-md, --lw-row-h, --lw-card-pad and --lw-stack-gap are what components paint with, and only those move.

It scopes, it does not globalise. data-density goes on a workspace, a grid, a rail — the same way brandVars() goes on the workspace and not on <html>. A dashboard is compact in its table and comfortable in its filter bar, and that is correct. The 44px touch minimum overrides density, set on the token rather than on each control, so it reaches every control including the ones not written yet.
