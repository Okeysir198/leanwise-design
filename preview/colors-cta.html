Accent ramp

Amber. One per view. At 43° it sits ~144° from the logo cyan — near-complementary, which is what keeps it legible as an accent rather than reading as a second brand color. The ramp is anchored on 400 — , the tier that carries amber as text on navy at :1; everything else is derived as a tint or shade of it. Values are read from tokens.css at render time.

Where the anchor applies. Dark themes point --lw-cta-text (and therefore the shadcn --cta-on / text-cta-on utility) at 400, light themes at 700 — same role, tier chosen by ground. Code syntax highlighting uses 400 for strings, tags and attribute values on the navy ground. Dark --lw-cta-soft (#36240D) is tuned so 400 sits on it at 9.22:1. 400 is the single amber-as-text tier system-wide; 500 is the fill and 600 its hover.

Amber is a light fill at every usable tier, so it always takes navy ink — the opposite of brand, which takes white. The swatches above pick their own ink by measurement, so that contrast is shown rather than claimed. 500 is the CTA fill and 600 its hover — both shades of the anchor. The 700 (not shown above) exists only for amber as text on white (:1); lemon at 55°+ cannot produce a 700 that holds AA there without turning olive.
