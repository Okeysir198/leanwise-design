Radii

Seven tiers on a 4px geometric scale: 2 · 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · pill. Every tier lands on the spacing grid, so an inner radius minus its padding is always another real token. Tight radii on controls and soft ones reserved for surfaces is what makes an interface read as software rather than as a consumer app. Hover a tier to isolate it.

The dashed inset above is the concentric rule: a child inset by p takes radius outer − p. On this scale that subtraction always lands on another tier — 12 minus 4 is 8, 16 minus 4 is 12. An arithmetic +4 run cannot do that.

The default is 8px, and it is what shadcn's --radius resolves to, so it sets the tone for every control in an app. Eight reads crisp; ten reads pillowy. Reserve 2xl for full-bleed surfaces — a soft radius on a control makes software look like a consumer app.
