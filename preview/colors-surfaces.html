The rule

  • Recede, don't relabel. --lw-bg-subtle/-muted/-inset are for grounds meant to sit under the content: the sidebar rail, a table head, an inset panel, a hover row, a chip. Anything that floats above the page — card, dialog, popover, toast, menu — takes --lw-bg and earns its edge from the hairline plus a shadow tier.
  • Distance is the whole design. The tiers stop at 5.8% below the page. Further than that and a "lift" becomes a second background colour, which is the single fastest way to make a clean layout look dirty. v1.13.0 shipped 2.5–7.1% at hue 45 and did exactly that.
  • One family. Paper is 38° on light and navy on dark; the ink hue is shared. A ground that picks its own grey — a cool #F6F8FB beside a warm page — reads as a different product, so template literals track these tokens rather than inventing neighbours.
